# Acme Robotics — Risk Factors

## Customer concentration

Three automotive customers account for roughly 60% of revenue. Losing any
one of them would materially reduce sales. Contracts are renewed annually
and none of the three has committed beyond the current model year.

## Supply chain

Precision servo motors are sourced from a single supplier in Japan. A
disruption at that supplier would delay production by several months.
Acme holds about six weeks of motor inventory and has not qualified a
second source.

## Competition

Larger competitors with lower prices are entering the mid-size arm segment.
Acme may have to cut prices or lose share. Two of those competitors have
announced Pittsburgh-area sales offices for next year.

## Labor

About a third of the assembly workforce is eligible to retire within five
years. Training a replacement technician takes roughly eighteen months.
